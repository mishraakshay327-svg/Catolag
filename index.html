✦ About Catolag

Catolag is a free, all-in-one smart calculator suite designed to make everyday calculations simple, fast, and beautiful. Whether you need a basic calculator, want to know your age down to the day, check your BMI, convert units, or calculate a loan EMI — Catolag has you covered.

🎯 Our Mission

To provide everyone with a powerful set of calculation tools that are completely free, no sign-up required, and accessible from any device anywhere in the world.

🛠️ What We Offer

  • Normal Calculator with keyboard support
  • Age Calculator — years, months, days & more
  • BMI Calculator with health status
  • Unit Converter — 6 categories
  • Percentage Calculator — 3 methods
  • EMI Loan Calculator with Indian format

🔒 Privacy Policy

Your privacy is extremely important to us. This policy explains how Catolag handles your information.

📋 What We Collect

When you first visit Catolag, we ask for your name and email address before granting access. This information is stored only in your browser's session storage and is never sent to any server.

🚫 What We Do NOT Do

  • We do not store your data on any server
  • We do not sell your information to anyone
  • We do not send you any spam or marketing emails
  • We do not use tracking cookies
  • We do not share your data with third parties

🧮 Calculator Data

All calculations you perform on Catolag happen entirely in your browser. No calculation data is ever transmitted or stored anywhere outside your device.

🔗 External Links

Catolag contains links to external websites (AI tools, loan platforms). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those external sites.
